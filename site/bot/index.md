# Uma Stable Match Discord bot: club applications for Umamusume clubs

> Recruit trainers without the spreadsheets.

Trainers apply with one click from your club's page. The bot opens a private ticket in your Discord server with their fan pace and history, and you accept or decline right there.

- **Set up your club:** [app.umastable.com/applications](https://app.umastable.com/applications). Only your club's leader in the game can add the bot.

## How it works

1. **Quick Apply.** Trainers sign in with Discord and allow it to add them to your server.
2. **A private ticket opens.** An `app-` channel that only the applicant, the club's leader and the bot can see, in your club's category if it has one.
3. **Decide with real numbers.** Pace, fans this month, all-time totals, averages and rank, then Accept or Decline.

## Good to know

- One pending application per club, and five a day per trainer.
- Every application for your club is listed on the web.
- The bot checks it has the access it needs before your club goes live.
