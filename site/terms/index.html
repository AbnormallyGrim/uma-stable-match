The rules for using Uma Stable Match.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. What this is

Uma Stable Match is an unofficial, non-commercial fan tool run by Abnormally Grim. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by uma.moe or Cygames, Inc. By using the site or signing in, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

2. Who can use it

You must meet Discord's minimum age to use any feature that needs a Discord account. Searching for clubs needs no account.

3. Accounts and linking a trainer

  • Sign in with your own Discord account only.
  • Link only a trainer ID that you own. We check this with a key in your in-game profile comment. Don't try to link or pretend to be someone else's trainer.
  • You can delete your account at any time, as described in the Privacy Policy.

4. What you may not do

  • Scrape the site or overload it or uma.moe through it, or get around rate limits and the human check.
  • Send spam, false or abusive applications, or harass other players.
  • Use applicants' details for anything other than reviewing their application.
  • Try to break into the service, or interfere with how it works.

5. Club leaders

  • Only the leader of a club in the game may link it and manage its applications.
  • You are responsible for your own Discord server and its rules. The bot only creates the application channels you've allowed it to.
  • We may unlink a club and remove the bot if it is being misused. You can unlink a club yourself at any time.

6. Discord

Applications create private channels on the club's own Discord server. That server is under Discord's terms and the club leader's control, not ours. We can't delete anything once it is in Discord.

7. Accuracy of the data

Club and trainer data comes from uma.moe and may be late or wrong. Recommendations are estimates to help you decide, not judgements of any player or club. We aren't responsible for who a club accepts or declines.

8. Availability

The service is free and run by one person. It may change, break or shut down at any time, without notice.

9. No warranty, and limited liability

The service is provided "as is", without promises of any kind. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for any loss that comes from using it, including a club you join or don't join because of a recommendation.

11. Ending access

We may block or remove access for anyone who breaks these terms. You can stop using the service, and delete your account, whenever you like.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the country where the operator is based.
